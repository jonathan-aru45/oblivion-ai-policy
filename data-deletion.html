Data Deletion Instructions

Request deletion of your data

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the Oblivion AI Assistant.

How to submit a request

  1. Email privacy@oblivionnn.tech with the subject Data Deletion Request.
  2. Identify the WhatsApp number/account used with the App. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or access tokens.
  3. State whether you want deletion of WhatsApp interaction data, connected-calendar data held by the App, or all data associated with your use of the App.
  4. We may ask for limited information to verify that the request relates to you before deleting data.

What we will delete

Where applicable and technically available, we will delete personal data controlled by the App, including retained workflow records or stored conversation data associated with the verified user, unless retention is required by law, security, fraud-prevention, or another legitimate obligation.

Connected services

Deleting App-controlled data does not automatically delete information held independently by Meta/WhatsApp, Microsoft, Google, Hostinger, or other providers. Users may also need to use those providers' own account, privacy, or deletion controls.

Revoke Microsoft access

If you connected Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365, you can revoke the App's authorization through your Microsoft account permissions/security settings. Revocation prevents future access but does not automatically erase records already created in your own Microsoft calendar.
