Oblivion AI Assistant

A WhatsApp-based AI assistant prototype that can receive messages, generate AI responses, and, when a user explicitly connects Microsoft Outlook, help perform calendar actions.

What the app can do

  • Receive text messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Generate responses using an AI model such as Google Gemini.
  • Optionally process voice notes through speech-to-text before AI processing.
  • Optionally connect to Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 Calendar to read, create, update, or delete calendar events when requested by the user.
  • Return the assistant's response through WhatsApp.
