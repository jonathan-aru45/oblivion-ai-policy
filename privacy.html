Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Oblivion AI Assistant (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when people use the App through WhatsApp and optional connected services such as Microsoft Outlook Calendar.

1. About the App

The App is a WhatsApp-based AI assistant prototype hosted using n8n automation infrastructure. A user can send a WhatsApp message to the App, the message can be processed by an AI model, and a response can be returned to the user through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Optional functionality may allow a user to connect Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 Calendar so the App can help with calendar tasks requested by the user. Optional voice-note support may also convert audio into text before AI processing.

2. Information we may process

  • WhatsApp account and messaging data: phone number or WhatsApp identifier, display/profile name when provided, message content, message IDs, timestamps, delivery/status metadata, and related webhook metadata.
  • AI conversation data: prompts, assistant responses, tool requests, and workflow context needed to provide the requested response.
  • Voice-note data: audio files or media identifiers and resulting transcripts, if voice-note functionality is enabled.
  • Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 data: only when a user intentionally connects a Microsoft account. This may include calendar names, event titles, dates and times, attendees, locations, descriptions, availability information, and event identifiers needed to perform requested calendar actions.
  • Technical data: request metadata, IP/network metadata made available to the server, error logs, execution logs, and service diagnostics.

3. How we use information

  • receive and respond to WhatsApp messages;
  • understand a user's request and generate an AI response;
  • perform a tool action specifically requested by the user, such as checking or changing a calendar event;
  • process a voice note into text when that feature is used;
  • maintain security, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and improve reliability; and
  • comply with legal obligations where applicable.

4. Microsoft Outlook Calendar access

The App does not access Microsoft Outlook Calendar unless the user connects a Microsoft account and authorizes the requested permissions. If calendar integration is enabled, the App may request permissions necessary to read calendar information and, where the user chooses action-capable functionality, create, update, or delete events.

The App is designed so that calendar actions are initiated in response to user instructions. Users can revoke Microsoft account access through their Microsoft account security/permissions settings at any time.

5. AI processing

Messages or transcripts may be sent to an AI model provider, currently expected to include Google Gemini API, to generate a response or determine which connected tool should be used. Information sent to an AI provider is subject to that provider's applicable terms, privacy commitments, and API settings.

Users should avoid sending highly sensitive, confidential, or regulated information through the App unless they are satisfied that the connected services and deployment configuration are appropriate for that information.

6. Service providers and integrations

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform for WhatsApp messaging and webhook delivery;
  • Google Gemini API for AI processing;
  • Microsoft / Microsoft Graph for optional Outlook and Microsoft 365 calendar functionality;
  • n8n for workflow automation and orchestration; and
  • Hostinger for hosting infrastructure and related web services.

These providers process data according to their own terms and privacy policies when their services are used.

7. Data retention

We aim to keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, troubleshoot issues, maintain security, or satisfy legal requirements. Workflow execution logs and diagnostic records may temporarily contain message or integration data depending on the n8n and server configuration.

Because this App is currently a test/development service, retention settings may change as the service is refined. We will update this Policy if we adopt a materially different retention practice.

8. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to the service providers listed above only as needed to operate the App or fulfill a user-requested action. We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to a development-stage application, including HTTPS transport and access-controlled service credentials. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. User choices and rights

Users may stop using the App at any time. Depending on the connected service, users may also revoke access directly through WhatsApp, Microsoft, Google, or other provider settings. Requests to access, correct, or delete information associated with the App can be made using the contact details below.

11. Data deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion are available at https://oblivionnn.tech/data-deletion.html.

12. Children's privacy

The App is not intentionally designed for children under the age required to independently consent to digital services in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information.

13. International processing

Because the App uses cloud and API providers, information may be processed in countries other than the user's country. Those providers may use data centers and subprocessors in multiple jurisdictions.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, providers, permissions, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will show when the latest revision was made.

15. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests can be sent to privacy@oblivionnn.tech.
